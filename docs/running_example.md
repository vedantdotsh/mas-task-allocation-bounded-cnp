# Verified running example

Generated by `mas_sim.example.write_example`; tested against the real simulator.

Three registered agents; one managed task arrives at time 4, at (6, 8), workload 6, required skill 0, no deadline. Pre-existing queue endpoints/finish times are known at registration. Default soft proficiency (theta=0); CNP k=2, fresh capability shortlist.

Bid = max(0, committed finish - now) + Euclidean distance / speed + workload / max(skill, 0.05). The capability shortlist uses only the last two terms, never private backlog.

| Agent | Position | Speed | Skill | Finish | Backlog | Travel | Execution | Registry score | Bid |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | [0.0, 0.0] | 2 | 0.5 | 10 | 6 | 5 | 12 | 17 | 23 |
| 1 | [6.0, 0.0] | 2 | 1.0 | 4 | 0 | 4 | 6 | 10 | 10 |
| 2 | [6.0, 8.0] | 1 | 0.25 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 24 | 24 | 24 |

Agent 0: distance sqrt(6²+8²)=10, travel=10/2=5; execution=6/0.5=12; backlog=10-4=6; bid=23. Agent 1: distance=8, travel=8/2=4; execution=6/1=6; backlog=0; bid=10. Agent 2: distance=0, travel=0; execution=6/0.25=24; backlog=0; bid=24.

Capability order is 1, 0, 2. Nearest order is 2, 1, 0. The k=2 capability bidders quote 10 and 23, so agent 1 wins. Its dispatch is max(4,4)=4, service starts at 4+4=8, completes at 8+6=14: wait=4, response=10. The registry receives position (6,8) and committed finish 14.

SSI/CBBA wait until the first 25-unit batch closes. They choose agent 1 at time 25, start service at 29, and finish at 35. Their response=31 includes batching. SSI uses live vector bids and CBBA live consensus; neither reads the registry after initialization or sends unused status updates.
